\begin{sigstatement}
High-speed atomic force microscopy (HS-AFM) enables real-time observation of biomolecular dynamics under near-physiological conditions, yet extracting three-dimensional atomic structures from surface topography images remains challenging.
Existing approaches face a trade-off: rigid-body fitting is fast but cannot capture conformational flexibility, while molecular dynamics-based flexible fitting captures structural changes but requires hours to days of computation per frame.
\Model overcomes this trade-off by combining a symmetry-aware neural network with guided diffusion-based structure generation, reconstructing physically plausible conformations in approximately one minute per frame without molecular dynamics simulations.
This speed enables high-throughput structural analysis of entire HS-AFM movies of hundreds of frames through AFM-conditioned generative reconstruction, yielding detailed structural insight into time-correlated domain motions that reflect underlying conformational dynamics.
\end{sigstatement}
